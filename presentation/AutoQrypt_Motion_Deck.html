01 // THE 20-YEAR SDV HORIZON

Why Classical OTA Signatures Fail on Modern Vehicles

THREAT 01 · SHOR'S CRQC

20-Year Road Lifespan

Software-Defined Vehicles operate for 15–20 years. Adversaries archiving wireless OTA traffic today can forge classical RSA/ECDSA signatures once quantum computers scale.

THREAT 02 · SINGLE-PRIMITIVE RISK

Pure PQC Is Untested Alone

Automotive safety standards resist replacing battle-tested classical curves with a single post-quantum primitive. AutoQrpt enforces a strict PQ/T Hybrid 2FA lock where both must pass.

THREAT 03 · SILICON CONSTRAINTS

520 KB SRAM & 64B CAN-FD

Naive PQC implementations overflow microcontroller task stacks and flood 64-byte CAN-FD frames. AutoQrpt verifies inside 36.5 KB stack and compresses Director auth into 11 frames.
